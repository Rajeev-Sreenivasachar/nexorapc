Frequently asked questions

How does the PC Builder work?

Each selection stores a product ID. JavaScript reads structured socket, memory, case, cooler, and power data to create compatibility results and calculate the demo total.

How do recommendations work?

The secure API asks Gemini to recommend only catalog products. If it is unavailable, a clearly labeled rule-based recommender uses budget tiers and compatible preset choices.

Can I buy products here?

No. Nexora is a student demonstration and has no order, payment, shipping, or live inventory system.

Does “compatible” guarantee a real build will work?

No. The checker covers a few structured rules, not BIOS versions, physical clearances, cables, regional power, or every manufacturer requirement. Always verify the official manuals before purchasing.
