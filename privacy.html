Plain-language policy

Privacy.

What this student demonstration stores, sends, and does not collect.

Browser storage

Nexora stores cart items, the current PC build, and named saved builds in your browser using localStorage. This data stays on the device and browser profile where it was created. You can remove it by clearing the cart, resetting or deleting builds, or clearing site data in your browser.

Recommendation assistant

When you use the AI Assistant and its serverless endpoint is configured, your message, budget, selected goal, stated use, existing-parts note, and a limited copy of the demonstration catalog are sent to Google’s Gemini API. Do not enter personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

Support form

The support form validates information in your browser and opens a draft in your own email application. Nexora does not receive or store the form. The draft has no recipient because the project currently has no published support address.

What is not implemented

Nexora has no accounts, payment processing, order database, advertising tracker, or analytics code in this repository. Hosting providers may still process ordinary request information according to their own policies.

Changes

This page should be updated if the team later adds analytics, accounts, a database, or another service that handles visitor data.
