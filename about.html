About Nexora

PC building should feel exciting, not confusing.

Nexora is a student-built PC shopping and planning platform designed to make components easier to understand, compare, and combine.

Our mission

Make every hardware decision clearer.

Model numbers, sockets, power requirements, and compatibility rules can turn a simple upgrade into hours of research. Nexora aims to bring the important details into one focused experience.

Our mission is to make PC building, component shopping, and hardware comparisons easier for beginners without removing the depth enthusiasts expect.

What makes us different

Built around the decision, not the sale.

01

Compatibility with context

Nexora explains what was checked, what failed, and what still needs manufacturer verification.

Honest information

What is real and what is a demo?

Nexora is an FBLA student project, not an operating retailer. Its planning tools, browser storage, compatibility logic, review form, and server-side AI connection are functional. Inventory, sales, checkout, payments, shipping, and the Thanksgiving discount are not active.
